## ⚙️ Manual Installation (Developer Mode)

Because these are independent tools, they are installed directly from your local drive. Setup takes 30 seconds.

1. **Download** the repository or the specific folder you want (e.g., `/Reelsy`).
2. **Open** your Chromium-based browser (Chrome, Edge, Arc, Brave) and navigate to `chrome://extensions`.
3. **Enable** **Developer mode** (usually a toggle in the top-right corner).
4. **Click** **"Load unpacked"** and select the folder you downloaded.
5. **Pin** the extension to your toolbar for quick access.

## 🔒 Privacy & Permissions

My extensions operate entirely within your browser's local sandbox. 
* **Zero Analytics:** No tracking pixels, no event logging, no third-party scripts.
* **Zero External Servers:** Everything runs locally on your machine.
* **Minimal Permissions:** Manifests strictly limit access to the exact URLs required to function (e.g., `instagram.com` for Reelsy). No global tab reading.
